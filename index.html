Tadaa Scheduler

Tadaa Scheduler is a personal social media scheduler. It lets its owner plan, schedule and publish their own videos and posts to their own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube) via each platform's official API, using a self-hosted copy of the open-source scheduler Postiz running on the owner's own computer.

This is a personal, single-user tool — not a public product or service.
